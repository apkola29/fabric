Lens: the Python tooling (slice B of a closure check of what round 5 changed, with the slices handed to different families than in round 5): the validator, the commit checker and the fact checker, and their tests. The reviewer had not seen an earlier round. It read a frozen copy of the published commit `f6ed0fc4de` and the diff of round 5, ran its probes in a temporary folder that it removed afterwards, and mutated the code to see which tests noticed. This is the reply condensed; locations refer to that commit. Probe strings that look like paths are described and not quoted. The disposition of each finding is in [round-6-findings.md](round-6-findings.md).

## Mutations tried

Twenty-one mutations beyond the author's 24. Four were caught (the segment-present check, the lower-casing of the final host, the strict boolean test of the archived flag, and the advisory identifier). Seventeen survived, as listed in R6-B-7.

## Checked and sound

- The ignore-rule engine on the ordinary shapes: trailing and leading spaces, CRLF endings, a trailing slash after a wildcard, negation under a folder or a wildcard, the same name as a file and as a folder, leading dots, and the four rules that are refused (`a/**`, `/`, `!`, `!/`). All matched Git.
- The hygiene of the project text: the baseline has no error and no warning, accented Latin letters, typographic punctuation and CRLF files pass, format characters and the non-BMP tag characters are refused, and a lone surrogate fails closed.
- `check_extra_file` on an empty file, a folder and a byte-order mark with a NUL: all fail closed or pass cleanly as they should.
- The `blob_hashes` rule accepts only the hash of the bytes and of the bytes with CRLF turned into LF.
- The two absence entries of the fact list use controls that a generic error page would not contain.

## Could not verify

Symbolic links on this account (four tests skip), a live GitHub call of the commit checker, and the two npm checks (blocked by a local TLS proxy).

Verdict from the reviewer: 1 high, 6 medium, 0 low; 17 of 21 new mutations survived.
