## 2. New Findings

### A3-1 — Medium — `agent-verifier` does not load `factory-standards`, contradicting `factory-standards/SKILL.md`, and `toolchain/README.md` omits `factory-standards` from the load-plan description

- **Where:** `toolchain/skills/factory-standards/SKILL.md`, lines 3, 16 and 23; `toolchain/instructions/factory-preamble.md`, lines 16 and 25; `toolchain/profiles.json`, lines 56–65; `scripts/toolchain.py`, lines 1480–1482; `toolchain/README.md`, lines 91.
- **What is wrong:** `toolchain/skills/factory-standards/SKILL.md`, lines 3 says to use the skill *"when planning, implementing, verifying or reviewing any change ... before a plan is written and again when a change is checked"*, `lines 16` tells the checker to *"go through the checks that apply and report each one by id as met, not met or not applicable"*, and `lines 23` defines `[review]` as *"a reviewer decides it from the change and its description"*. However, `scripts/toolchain.py`, lines 1480–1482 attaches `repo_skill_folders(folder)` only when `profile["skills"] or profile["mcp"]` is non-empty. Because `agent-verifier` ("Judges the diff against the spec and the sanitized evidence", `toolchain/profiles.json`, lines 56–65) has `"skills": []` and `"mcp": []`, its load plan has `"repo_skills": []`, and the C2-3 fix in `toolchain/instructions/factory-preamble.md`, lines 16 tells it: *"A profile without the `factory-router` or `factory-standards` skill works from these rules alone."* Consequently, the job role that verifies/reviews a change never receives the vendored 89-check standards pack. Separately, `toolchain/README.md`, lines 91 ("How a job uses it") still describes the load plan as listing *"the skills of this repository (the router)"*, omitting `factory-standards`.
- **Evidence:** Ran from `CLONE\fabric-automation`:
  ```powershell
  python scripts/toolchain.py render --profile agent-verifier --host load-plan
  ```
  Observed `"skills": []`, `"repo_skills": []`, and `"instructions": [{"path": "instructions/factory-preamble.md", ...}]`, whereas `agent-planner` and `agent-test-author` receive `"repo_skills": [{"name": "factory-router", ...}, {"name": "factory-standards", ...}]`.
- **Suggested fix:** Either include `factory-standards` in `agent-verifier`'s load plan (it is static markdown guidance, not a tool or server) or state in `toolchain/skills/factory-standards/SKILL.md`, lines 3 and 16 which roles load it; and in `toolchain/README.md`, lines 91, change `"(the router)"` to `"(the router and the standards)"`.

### A3-2 — Low — Whole-request escalation for `[flag]` dependencies is in `factory-standards/SKILL.md` only, not in `AGENTS.md` or `factory-preamble.md`

- **Where:** `AGENTS.md`, lines 29; `toolchain/instructions/factory-preamble.md`, lines 16 and 25; `toolchain/skills/factory-standards/SKILL.md`, lines 24 and 48.
- **What is wrong:** To fix C2-1, `toolchain/skills/factory-standards/SKILL.md`, lines 24 added: *"Flag it to a person with the id and the source, and say what in the change touches it. If the request itself needs such a change, for example row-level security for HR data, do not build the rest without it: say so and escalate the whole request to a person."* By contrast, the C2-5 fix in `AGENTS.md`, lines 29 and `toolchain/instructions/factory-preamble.md`, lines 25 says only: *"A change to security, permissions, a network setting, a sensitivity label or a tenant setting is flagged to a person with the id of the check, and never made."* An agent working from a profile without `factory-standards` (`toolchain/instructions/factory-preamble.md`, lines 16) or reading only `AGENTS.md` or `factory-preamble.md` is not told to escalate the *entire* request rather than building an unprotected item without its required RLS, permission, or sensitivity label, nor to cite the check's source alongside its id.
- **Evidence:** Compare `AGENTS.md`, lines 29 and `toolchain/instructions/factory-preamble.md`, lines 25 with `toolchain/skills/factory-standards/SKILL.md`, lines 24 and 48.
- **Suggested fix:** In `AGENTS.md`, lines 29 and `toolchain/instructions/factory-preamble.md`, lines 25, add `"and its source, and never made; if the request itself needs such a change, escalate the whole request without building the rest"`.
