- `std-sem-04` Does the definition show database compatibility level 1604 or higher, and Direct Lake partitions in directLake mode? [gate] Applies to: the definition of a new or changed Direct Lake semantic model. Source: Direct Lake develop, "Direct Lake model metadata".

F2 | Medium | note-eng-2 | use session overrides sparingly for debugging | The note states that rule, then says the pages give no rule for dividing settings between the environment and %%configure. "Configuration hierarchy" does give that rule.

Source: Spark monitoring, "Configuration hierarchy"; Spark monitoring, "The %%configure magic command"; Spark development, "Scenario: Customize session configuration in the Notebook".

F3 | Medium | std-cap-12 | Many interactive operations like SQL query | The cited surge-protection bullets say many interactive operations, such as a SQL query, are also considered background and might be rejected. An agent flagging surge protection is not told that.
Suggested replacement:
- `std-cap-12` Flag a surge protection change; guidance: while active it rejects background jobs, which can be delayed or need reruns; it does not replace optimization or scaling. [flag] Applies to: a surge protection setting of a capacity; many interactive operations, such as a SQL query, are also considered background and might be rejected, and the optimize page recommends isolating critical solutions in a correctly sized capacity. Source: Optimize capacity, "Configure surge protection"; Manage growth, "Enterprise (centralized) capacities".

F4 | Low | std-cap-13 | make sure the capacity isn't in use | The check keeps the billing event, unavailable content, and the three-times rate, but drops the page's precondition that you first make sure the capacity is not in use.

[flag] Applies to: pausing, or overage billing, on an F SKU capacity. Source: Throttling, "How to stop throttling when it occurs".
