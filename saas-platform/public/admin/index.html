      Fabric as the data platform behind a SaaS app

      Give every customer their own analytics workspace

      Each HiCRM customer gets a Fabric workspace of their own, run by a service account that can reach nothing else. Their CRM data lives in a Fabric SQL database; reports and the assistant read it through one semantic model. The customer only ever sees HiCRM.

      1. Provision. Workspace, the customer's service account, the CRM database, the HiCRM Insights model bound to a workspace identity, and the assistant.
      2. Use. The CRM writes to SQL; Fabric replicates it to OneLake and the model reads it directly (Direct Lake), so reports stay current without copy jobs.
      3. Analyze. Embedded reports with short-lived tokens, report building with a describe-a-chart box, and an assistant that answers from the model.
